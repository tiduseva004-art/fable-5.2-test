Claude Fable 5.2 shipped on 2026-11-09. Within days X filled up with games, 3D worlds and web pages made with it. This list collects the ones **you can open right now**: 104 live works, every link opened and checked on 2026-11-12, plus 87 popular video-only demos in a separate section.

Every entry has a few-second GIF preview, credits its creator and links back to the original post for the full video. 🆕 marks entries added in the latest update. If this helps, a ⭐ keeps it coming — new works are still appearing daily.

## 📰 News

- **2026-11-12**: +47 live works and +26 video demos (marked 🆕), now 104 + 87. All links re-checked. Previews are about one seventh of their old size (about 11 MB for the page), so it loads much faster.
- **2026-11-10**: Every entry now has a GIF preview.
- **2026-11-10**: First release — 57 live works + 61 video demos.

## 🧭 How This List Was Made

- 34 searches on X (Top + Latest, English/Chinese/Japanese/Korean keywords), then the recent posts of 331 creators, so links hidden in self-replies were recovered too.
- 2026-11-12 update: re-read those creators' new posts, cross-checked about 600 posts from other Fable 5.2 lists, ran 12 fresh X searches limited to posts after Nov 10, and read the posts of 300 more creators.
- Every external link was requested on 2026-11-12 and returned HTTP 200; claude.ai artifacts were spot-checked for content and attribution.
- Press coverage, "now available on X" ads, tool repos and reshares were removed. Credit goes to the original creator; a resharer is noted only when the creator posted no link.
- GIF previews: a 1.5-second clip from the creator's own video, or a short headless-browser capture of the live site when the post had no video. About 55 KB each, about 11 MB for the whole page.
- Entries mentioning "Fable 5.5" keep the creator's own name for the pre-release routed model.
